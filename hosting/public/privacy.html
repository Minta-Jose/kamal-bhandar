Privacy policy

Kamal Bhandar app · version 2026-10

Kamal Bhandar is a grocery store. Its Android app lets approved customers order groceries for pickup at the store. This policy explains what the app keeps and why, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your first and last name, mobile number and address, which you type in when you register.
  • The email address of the Google account you sign in with.
  • The orders you place and their status.
  • A notification token for your phone, so we can tell you when your order is ready.
  • Crash reports, which contain technical details about the phone but not your personal details.

Why we collect it

To check that you are a real customer before approving your account, to prepare your orders, to contact you about them, and to keep the store's accounts. We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell or share it.

Where it is stored

In Google Firebase, in Google's data centre in Mumbai, India. Only the store owner can see customer details.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your name, mobile, address, cart and notification tokens are removed. Past orders are kept for the store's accounts without your name, mobile or address.

Your rights

You can see and correct your name and address in the app's Profile tab, and delete your account at any time from Profile. To change your mobile number, or for any question or complaint about your data, contact the store owner using the details shown in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will ask you to agree to the new version.
