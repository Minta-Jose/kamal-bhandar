Delete your account

Kamal Bhandar app

In the app

  1. Open the Kamal Bhandar app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Without the app

Visit or call the store and ask the owner to delete your account. They will do it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your name, mobile number, address, cart and notification tokens are deleted straight away, along with your sign-in account. Past orders are kept for the store's accounts without your name, mobile or address.
